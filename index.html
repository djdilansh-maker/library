About Emmanuel Senior Secondary School

Emmanuel Senior Secondary School, located in Tonk (PIN 304001), is committed to providing quality education and holistic development. The school focuses on academic excellence, moral values, and extracurricular growth. Our motto "God With Us" guides our work with students, parents, and the community to build responsible citizens of the future.

Academics & Co-curricular

We offer classes through primary to senior secondary level, with a strong emphasis on science and computer education. Students are encouraged to participate in sports, arts, debate, and technology clubs. Experienced teachers and modern classrooms support student learning and critical thinking.

Library & Projects

The school library houses physical books across subjects and supports student projects such as the "Library Management System" developed using Python and MySQL. For the project code and live demo, visit our GitHub repository (link below).
